# Anticipatory action framework

Sprint 3.23 | Design proposal | No operational trigger validated

The framework specifies candidate actions for institutional review. All owners, service standards, programme rules and funding commitments remain unassigned or subject to verification. Candidate indicators initiate review; they do not authorize payment or establish AI causation.

## Decision record required for every action

Record the source and vintage, signal and uncertainty, assessed need, eligibility decision and reasons, responsible reviewer, budget authorization, delivery deadline and actual receipt, consent or other applicable basis, appeal route, review date and exit decision. Keep a record of excluded and failed cases as well as successful delivery.

## A01 Benefit navigation

Candidate indicators: Claim processing time; incomplete applications; unresolved claims.

Evidence before authorization: Confirm applicable benefit rules and document actual access barriers.

Authority: Unassigned programme reviewer and budget holder.

Eligibility questions: Who is entitled; what documentation is proportionate; what help is available to excluded applicants.

Finance formula: Approved cases times navigation unit cost plus setup and accessible communication.

Cost basis: Engine anchor CAD 150 per participant; not a procurement estimate.

Delivery lead time: Measure referral to completed application and actual first receipt; compare with assessed gap date.

Review and appeal: Written reasons; accessible correction and independent review; service standard unassigned.

Monitoring: Receipt delay, unsuccessful applications, participant time, complaints.

Evaluation: Compare receipt timing and hardship against credible comparison; do not infer earnings effects.

Risks: Exclusion through documentation; accelerated receipt may not reduce all gap measures.

Exit condition: Claim resolved or referred; review persistent barriers.

## A02 Temporary income bridge

Candidate indicators: Verified earnings interruption; near term essential spending gap.

Evidence before authorization: Aligned household needs and income definitions; benefit interaction review; payment feasibility.

Authority: Unassigned authorized funder and delivery manager.

Eligibility questions: Which unit receives support; residence and programme rules; duplication; accessibility.

Finance formula: Eligible recipients times weekly transfer times approved weeks plus administration and contingency.

Cost basis: Engine anchor CAD 180 weekly for 12 weeks; illustrative only.

Delivery lead time: Approve only with measured payment capacity; receipt must precede anticipated gap to count as anticipatory.

Review and appeal: Explain award amount and duration; correction channel; review before termination.

Monitoring: Time to receipt, unmet essentials, payment failure, exclusion and repeat need.

Evaluation: Prespecified hardship and sustained earnings outcomes; causal comparison and benefit interaction assessment.

Risks: Benefit offset; unsafe data collection; unsuitable fixed amount; abrupt ending.

Exit condition: Reassess need before planned end; taper or referral only under approved rules.

## A03 Conditional job retention

Candidate indicators: Employer adjustment plans; reduced hours; credible threatened layoffs.

Evidence before authorization: Independent programme eligibility confirmation; business viability and worker agreement review.

Authority: Unassigned programme authority with employer and worker representation.

Eligibility questions: Is employer eligible; which workers; hours limits; wage and benefit protections.

Finance formula: Eligible worker weeks times applicable benefit plus administration, employer cost and approved training.

Cost basis: Engine anchors CAD 120 administration, CAD 250 employer cost and CAD 400 training per participant; not current scheme costing.

Delivery lead time: Lead time includes employer application, agreement and payment; verify before reductions occur.

Review and appeal: Worker accessible complaint route separate from employer; programme appeal route to be confirmed.

Monitoring: Actual hours, earnings, exits, displacement of nonparticipants and job quality.

Evaluation: Estimate sustained employment and net earnings relative to eligible comparison; test deadweight.

Risks: Subsidizing jobs that would remain; delayed inevitable exit; employer pressure.

Exit condition: Scheduled eligibility and viability review; referral when retention no longer appropriate.

## A04 Skills and job matching

Candidate indicators: Destination vacancies; skill requirements; worker preferences; training completion barriers.

Evidence before authorization: Evidence of destination demand, training quality and participant ability to attend.

Authority: Unassigned employment service commissioner.

Eligibility questions: Which skills are relevant; language access; care, transport and credential barriers.

Finance formula: Participants times training and support cost plus matching and follow up.

Cost basis: Engine anchors CAD 1300 training and CAD 100 worker expense; assumed acceleration is not evidence.

Delivery lead time: Measure enrolment, completion and job entry separately; provide income support if required.

Review and appeal: Choice of provider; correction of assessment; accessible complaint and withdrawal process.

Monitoring: Completion, job entry, sustained net earnings, hours and job quality.

Evaluation: Measure sustained earnings and wellbeing; compare with credible alternative; include opportunity cost.

Risks: Training without demand; debt or unpaid time; poor placement quality.

Exit condition: Completion or informed withdrawal; follow up and alternative referral.

## A05 Needs assessment and referral

Candidate indicators: Self reported unmet essentials; service requests; verified access barriers.

Evidence before authorization: Consent; suitable assessment; available referral capacity and eligibility.

Authority: Unassigned case management lead and receiving service.

Eligibility questions: What needs exist now; which services are accessible; what information is necessary.

Finance formula: Cases times assessment and referral cost plus interpretation and accessibility.

Cost basis: No validated unit cost; obtain local costing before approval.

Delivery lead time: Measure first contact to completed service receipt; urgent current need follows appropriate response pathway.

Review and appeal: Allow correction, service choice and escalation; avoid repeated intrusive assessment.

Monitoring: Completed referrals, unresolved need, service refusal and participant burden.

Evaluation: Assess resolved needs and adverse effects; distinguish referral offered from service received.

Risks: Privacy loss; unavailable services; stigmatization; administrative burden.

Exit condition: Confirmed resolution or documented safe handover with consent.

## A06 Monitoring and decision review

Candidate indicators: Employment, wages, vacancies, payment delays and hardship signals with vintage labels.

Evidence before authorization: Defined coverage, data quality, revision handling and prespecified review process.

Authority: Unassigned analytical lead; action decisions remain with authorized programme owner.

Eligibility questions: Whose data are represented; which populations are missing; permissible data use.

Finance formula: Staff time plus data access, secure systems and evaluation budget.

Cost basis: No approved operating budget; quote costs before commitment.

Delivery lead time: Measure source lag and decision lag against useful action window; no assumed positive lead time.

Review and appeal: Document signal challenges and corrections; no automated denial; review disputed case data.

Monitoring: False alarms, missed cases, lead time, missingness, differential exclusion and decision outcomes.

Evaluation: Prospective evaluation with frozen rules and independent outcomes before trigger validation.

Risks: Publication delays; spurious attribution; unrepresentative data; surveillance creep.

Exit condition: Suspend an unreliable indicator; stop monitoring when purpose or lawful basis expires.

## Readiness and evaluation

Prepare accessible materials and referral arrangements before a shock. Progress to individual review only with suitable data and a responsible institution. Authorize an action only with verified rules, budget and delivery capacity. Monitor actual receipt and outcomes; reopen, correct or stop a decision when evidence changes. No numerical trigger or analytical deposit band is an approved eligibility threshold.

Before a pilot, prespecify the population, outcomes, follow-up, comparison design, analysis, adverse-event handling and data retention. Estimate false alarms and missed cases where a warning rule is tested. Distinguish simulation uncertainty, sampling uncertainty and causal identification. Programme benefit, fiscal saving and societal net are different quantities.
