# Canada COI AET executive brief

Sprint 3.23 | 29 September 2026 | Decision brief for research and implementation partners

Proceed to the final release audit of the Canadian research prototype. The project now provides descriptive evidence, transparent scenario accounting and a specified action framework. It does not yet support automated targeting, a validated warning trigger or a claim that the simulated interventions cause the displayed returns.

## Three findings that matter

Employee earnings: the August 2026 public LFS analysis estimates mean hourly earnings of CAD 37.02, with an approximate 95% interval of CAD 36.80 to 37.25. This is an unadjusted employee mean, not disposable household income. [S01]

Financial vulnerability: the 2023 SFS analysis estimates a financially difficult missed-payment prevalence of 5.55%. The prevalence is 9.45% for positive deposits below CAD 5,000 and 1.51% for deposits of at least CAD 20,000. The 7.94 percentage point difference is an association; hardship may have depleted deposits. It does not estimate the impact of a cash transfer. [S02]

Labour context: the inherited 2026Q2 summary shows modest employment and vacancy growth alongside stronger nominal wage growth, while five broad groups lost employment. The results do not establish AI-caused displacement. [S06]

## What the scenario comparison contributes

For a simulated population scaled to 1,000 workers, C1 navigation adds about CAD 58,000 fiscal outlay; C2 conditional retention saves about CAD 999,000; and C3 bridge support with assumed faster job return adds about CAD 610,000. C1 also increases below-floor weeks despite reducing a cash-deficit measure. These are conditional demonstrations with assumed effects, mixed-year CAD and an illustrative needs floor. Fiscal, household and societal results must remain separate. [S07]

## Recommended decision

Complete Sprint 3.24 and close Phase 1 as a documented research and scenario prototype. Do not expand the model to create an appearance of readiness. Any operational pilot needs a separately approved evaluation, programme authority, funding, eligibility rules and delivery arrangements.

# Action and release requirements

The proposed action framework covers benefit navigation, temporary income bridging, conditional retention, skills and job matching, needs referral, and monitoring. Each action has an evidence requirement, candidate cost formula, delivery condition, review route and evaluation question. All institutional owners are currently unassigned.

## Before any operational pilot

Confirm current programme rules and actual access for the intended cohort. Measure how quickly support can reach people and whether it can arrive before the gap. Establish a consent and data-protection process, an accessible appeal route, budget authority and a credible comparison design. Neither occupational exposure nor the CAD 5,000 analytical resource band is an authorized activation threshold.

The six blocked claims are validated transition forecasts, calibrated household risk, causal intervention returns, operational triggers, exact occupational exposure and humanitarian-risk probabilities. Broad immigrant categories do not establish results for refugees or asylum seekers. These limits remain visible in all outputs.

## The remaining sprint

Sprint 3.24 will reproduce selected results from pinned inputs, reconcile the complete known archive, verify the live website and download links, and publish the release handover. A missing resource or failed verification must be reported as a blocker. The finish line is Phase 1 release; a validated operational system would require a new research phase.
